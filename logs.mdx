title: 'Logs Management'
description: 'How to view, send, or delete your session logs'
---

Every run creates a session log. These are the first thing support will ask for if something goes wrong.

You don't need to find or manage these by hand, use the **Logs Management** menu instead.

## Logs Management

From the [main menu](/main-menu):

```
Logs Management:
  Send Logs
  Delete Logs
  Read Log
  ⌂  Main Menu
```

- **Send Logs**: pick one or more sessions, including your current one, and send them straight to Lumi for support. They're encrypted the moment they're sent, only Lumi can read them.
- **Delete Logs**: pick which sessions to permanently remove from your computer. This can't be undone.
- **Read Log**: pick a session and view it yourself. It opens in Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac), and the bot waits for you to close it before you can do anything else.
